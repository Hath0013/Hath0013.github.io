Favourite design resources 🎨

Last updated: Feb 25, 2026

Sharing is caring. Here are some of the MOST valuable resources I've discovered on my design journey. I'll continually update this list, so do check back every once in a while!

Accessibility

  • Color contrast checker: Easy way to ensure your text is WCAG compliant.
  • Eightshapes contrast grid: Best way to test many hex codes for WCAG 2.0 min contrast.
  • WebAIM contrast checker: An alternative colour contrast checker tool.

Colour

  • Color Blender: Blend one hex to another.
  • ColorPal: Chrome/Edge extension for easy browser hex-picking.
  • ColorSpace: Generates a bunch of different palettes from one hex.
  • Shade Generator: Throws shade.
  • Tint & Shade Generator: When you need quick tints of a hex code.

Imagery

  • GoFullPage: Chrome extension for full page screen capture with customizable settings.
  • Ruler Measurement: Easy, quick pixel dimensions for digital components.

Typography & Fonts

  • WhatFont: Chrome extension for the easiest way to identify fonts on web pages.
