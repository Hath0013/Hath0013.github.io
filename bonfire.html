Bonfire Interactive

A Euna Solutions product line.

Partnering cross-functionally with the Digital and Content teams, I delivered compelling end-to-end creative for Bonfire's 2023 SOPs report campaign. I led the visual direction and design execution for this campaign, effectively translating complex data into a cohesive visual narrative across all touchpoints.

Key Program Results 🔥
  • Highest report downloads in program history. Visually compelling design drove high engagement.
  • Overwhelmingly positive feedback. The program and campaign data proved major success.
  • Achieved a 5.5× increase in landing page conversion rate year over year. Results-driven design.
Design Parameters 📐
  • Easy to digest. Complex data needed to be easy to understand.
  • Retrofuturism theme. Incorporate retrofuturism while staying on brand.
  • Accommodate dense content. Use whitespace to balance heavy data.
  • A polished visual experience. Deliver visually compelling and effective design.
